Problem Statement
Current cyber forensic investigations require significant manual effort to analyze logs, correlate evidence, reconstruct attack timelines, and generate investigation reports. Existing tools lack autonomous reasoning capabilities and struggle with fragmented evidence sources. This research proposes ForensicGPT-Agent, a Multi-Agent LLM-based framework that autonomously reconstructs digital crime scenes, correlates forensic artifacts, identifies attack patterns, and generates explainable forensic reports to assist investigators.
